==================================================

1. PRIMARY GAME EXPERIENCE
   ==================================================

PREDICTED is a minimalist escape game.

The player is a human trapped inside an AI-controlled sequence of rooms.

The player is represented by a glowing dot.

Each room is a level.

The objective of every room is:

REACH THE ESCAPE SCORE TARGET BEFORE:

* TRACE reaches 100
* OR the 60-second timer reaches 0

When the player reaches the room's target score:

* the EXIT opens
* the player dot visibly moves toward the exit
* play a door-opening sound
* show a brief "YOU ESCAPED" sequence
* save progression
* unlock the next room

IMPORTANT:
If a player has unlocked Room 5 and fails inside Room 5, they must restart Room 5, NOT Room 1.

Progress must persist.

6. AI PREDICTION → REWARD LINK
==============================

THIS IS A CRITICAL FIX.

The AI prediction and reward generation must be causally connected.

Every turn must follow:

1. Analyze player behaviour.
2. Predict next direction.
3. Calculate prediction confidence.
4. Generate reward values.
5. The predicted direction MUST have the highest base reward.

Example:

AI prediction:
LEFT

Confidence:
84%

Rewards:
LEFT +72
CENTER +41
RIGHT +24

The reward generator must NEVER independently randomize values in a way that puts a different direction above the AI prediction.

The player must see:

"The AI expects LEFT, therefore LEFT is the biggest temptation."

7. VARIABLE REWARD VALUES
=========================

Do not use the same reward numbers repeatedly.

The current prototype appears to use largely stagnant reward values.

Fix this.

Use level-specific configurable ranges and controlled variation.

Examples:

52 / 35 / 22
64 / 42 / 26
58 / 37 / 24
73 / 44 / 29
67 / 38 / 21
78 / 46 / 25
83 / 47 / 31

These are examples, not fixed values.

The exact values should vary each turn while remaining within level-specific balance ranges.

The predicted direction must always be the highest base reward.

The reward spread should also depend on prediction confidence.

LOW confidence:
rewards relatively close together.

HIGH confidence:
predicted reward significantly higher than alternatives.

Example low confidence:
LEFT +54
CENTER +48
RIGHT +42

Example high confidence:
LEFT +82
CENTER +37
RIGHT +21

This makes AI confidence mechanically meaningful.

9. BLUFF SYSTEM
===============

A BLUFF occurs when:

AI confidence >= 65%

AND

the player selects a non-predicted direction.

When a successful bluff occurs:

* award the chosen direction's normal lower score
* award a small BLUFF bonus
* reduce Trace slightly
* increase bluff streak
* show an animated popup

Example:

"YOU FOOLED ME."

"+24"

"BLUFF +10"

BLUFF bonuses must remain smaller than the main predicted reward.

Suggested progression:

First consecutive bluff: +10
Second: +12
Third: +15
Fourth+: cap at +20

Do not allow bluffing to become the dominant scoring strategy.

The player should never discover:
"Always bluff = fastest progression."

==================================================
10. TRACE
=========

Trace represents how much the AI has successfully learned.

Trace increases when:

* player confirms predictions
* player repeatedly chooses the same direction
* player repeatedly chooses the highest-value reward
* player demonstrates predictable sequences

Trace decreases modestly when:

* player successfully bluffs a sufficiently confident prediction

Trace must respond to actual behaviour.

It must NOT be random.

When Trace reaches 100:
THE AI FIGURED YOU OUT.

The current room attempt ends.

IMPORTANT:
The player returns to the beginning of the CURRENT UNLOCKED ROOM, not Room 1.
