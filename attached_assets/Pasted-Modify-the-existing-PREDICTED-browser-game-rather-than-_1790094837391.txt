Modify the existing PREDICTED browser game rather than starting from scratch.

This is a focused redesign of the gameplay loop.

CORE EXPERIENCE

PREDICTED is a minimalist browser escape game.

The player is a human represented by a small glowing dot trapped inside an AI-controlled room.

Each room is a level.

The AI controls the room by observing the player's behaviour, predicting the player's next movement direction, and deliberately making its predicted direction the most rewarding option.

The player's objective is:

REACH THE ROOM'S TARGET SCORE AND ESCAPE BEFORE THE AI FULLY FIGURES YOU OUT.

The game is therefore about:

GREED vs DECEPTION.

The player wants immediate score but must remain unpredictable.

ROOM STRUCTURE

Each level is represented visually as an AI-controlled corridor/room with three movement lanes:

LEFT
CENTER
RIGHT

The player is represented by a dot.

Whenever the player chooses LEFT, CENTER, or RIGHT:

* the dot visibly moves toward that lane
* then moves forward through the room
* the room/corridor advances visually
* the next decision state appears

The physical movement is primarily visual feedback for the decision system; do not build complicated physics.

At the end of each room is an EXIT.

The EXIT begins locked.

When the player reaches the target score, the EXIT becomes unlocked and the dot should visibly move toward it.

GAME OBJECTIVE

Every room has:

* a target SCORE
* a TRACE meter
* a 60-second timer

For the first three levels:

LEVEL 1:
Target = 800

LEVEL 2:
Target = 1100

LEVEL 3:
Target = 1500

If SCORE reaches the room target:

* level is completed
* EXIT opens
* show a brief escape animation
* show room completion statistics
* proceed to the next level

If TRACE reaches 100:

* show "THE AI FIGURED YOU OUT"
* current run ends
* return to Level 1

If the timer reaches 0 before the target score is reached:

* show "TIME EXPIRED"
* current run ends
* return to Level 1

IMPORTANT:
Normal run failure resets the complete run.

ROOM-SPECIFIC CHALLENGE FAILURE, introduced after Level 3, resets only the current room.

CORE TURN

Every decision turn follows exactly this order:

1. Read the player model.
2. Generate the AI prediction.
3. Generate the confidence.
4. Generate the reward values based on the prediction and confidence.
5. Display the prediction and reward values.
6. Allow player input.
7. Move the player dot.
8. Award score.
9. Change TRACE.
10. Award BLUFF bonus if applicable.
11. Update the player model.
12. Update personality statistics.
13. Generate the next decision.

The reward system MUST NOT be generated independently of the prediction.

PREDICTION-REWARD RELATIONSHIP

The predicted direction MUST always be the highest-value base reward.

Example:

AI:
"I THINK YOU'LL GO LEFT"

Confidence:
84%

Rewards:

LEFT +65
CENTER +35
RIGHT +20

The player should clearly understand that the AI is tempting them with the option it believes they will choose.

CONFIDENCE AND REWARD SPREAD

AI confidence should affect reward spread.

Low confidence example:

LEFT +50
CENTER +44
RIGHT +38

High confidence example:

LEFT +85
CENTER +30
RIGHT +18

The exact values can vary within sensible limits.

The predicted direction must always have the highest base reward.

FOLLOWING THE PREDICTION

If the player follows the predicted direction:

* award the full predicted reward
* increase TRACE
* display feedback such as:

"I KNEW YOU WOULD."

"+65 SCORE"

"TRACE +10"

Trace increase should be influenced by prediction confidence.

Higher-confidence confirmations should increase TRACE more.

BLUFF SYSTEM

A BLUFF happens when:

AI confidence >= 65%

AND

the player chooses a direction other than the prediction.

When a successful bluff occurs:

* award the chosen direction's normal lower reward
* award a small BLUFF bonus
* reduce TRACE slightly
* display a prominent temporary feedback popup:

"YOU FOOLED ME"

"BLUFF +10"

The BLUFF bonus must remain smaller than the normal predicted reward.

Suggested bonus progression:

First consecutive successful bluff:
+10

Second:
+12

Third:
+15

Fourth and later:
cap at +20

Do not allow bluff bonuses to become the dominant scoring strategy.

BLUFF bonus should slightly increase the player's sense of mastery, while the primary score progression still comes from high-value choices.

TRACE SYSTEM

TRACE represents how much the AI has learned about the player.

TRACE should increase when:

* player confirms a prediction
* player repeats behaviours
* player consistently chooses highest-value rewards
* player demonstrates consistent directional patterns

TRACE should decrease slightly when:

* player successfully bluffs a sufficiently confident prediction

TRACE must be behaviour-driven rather than random.

PLAYER PROFILE

Track at minimum:

GREED:
percentage of choices where player selects the highest available reward

REPETITION:
percentage of repeated direction choices

RISK:
frequency of selecting lower-value options

BLUFF:
successful high-confidence prediction reversals

SEQUENCE:
transition tendencies between directions

At the end of every room show a compact player profile.

Possible archetypes:

THE GREEDY
THE REPEATER
THE GAMBLER
THE CONTRARIAN
THE OPPORTUNIST
THE UNPREDICTABLE

The profile must come from actual statistics rather than random assignment.

AI EVOLUTION

LEVEL 1 — OBSERVER

AI mainly learns:

* favourite direction
* recent direction frequency

Target:
800

Purpose:
Teach the player that the AI is observing them.

LEVEL 2 — READER

AI additionally learns:

* transitions
* recent sequences

Example:
After LEFT, the player tends to choose RIGHT.

Target:
1100

Purpose:
Teach the player that the AI remembers patterns.

LEVEL 3 — PROFILER

AI additionally uses:

* greed
* repetition
* risk
* bluff frequency

Target:
1500

Purpose:
Teach the player:
"The AI understands how I behave."

After Level 3, introduce the Challenge System.

LEVEL 4 — ADVERSARY

Target:
2000

AI should begin accounting for deliberate bluffing/contrarian behaviour.

Do not make the AI omniscient.

It must remain imperfect and beatable.

AI CONSTRAINT PHILOSOPHY

The AI should not feel like it is cheating.

Its power should come from:

* better observation
* stronger pattern recognition
* more dangerous temptations
* adaptive reward structures
* increasingly restrictive room challenges

It should NOT:

* secretly remove valid player choices
* change outcomes after the player chooses
* force random failure
* become 100% accurate

The player's decisions must always remain meaningful.

CHALLENGE SYSTEM

Beginning after Level 3, every room includes a room challenge.

A challenge is simultaneously:

* an OPPORTUNITY for bonus rewards
* a THREAT because violating its rule causes the current room to fail

Challenge success grants bonus points.

Challenge failure immediately ends the current room and restarts that room.

Do not reset the whole run when a challenge is failed.

Example Challenge 1:

"DON'T GET COMFORTABLE"

Rule:
The player cannot perform three consecutive successful BLUFFS.

If the player reaches a third consecutive successful bluff:
CHALLENGE FAILED
restart current room.

If the player completes the room without violating the rule:
+40 Challenge Bonus

Example Challenge 2:

"GREED IS VISIBLE"

Rule:
The player cannot choose the highest-value direction three times consecutively.

Success:
+50

Failure:
restart current room.

Example Challenge 3:

"PLAY ALONG"

Requirement:
Successfully follow two high-confidence predictions in the room.

Success:
+45 Challenge Bonus.

During this challenge, prediction confirmations increase TRACE more strongly.

Example Challenge 4:

"FALSE CONFIDENCE"

Requirement:
Successfully bluff at least one prediction above 80% confidence.

Success:
+45

Example Challenge 5:

"DON'T REPEAT YOURSELF"

Rule:
Cannot choose the same direction three times consecutively.

Success:
+35

Challenge difficulty must scale with room level.

Challenges should directly interact with:

* BLUFF frequency
* TRACE
* reward risk
* player personality
* AI prediction confidence

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

Keep the project:
index.html
style.css
script.js

Use vanilla JavaScript.

No framework is required for this prototype.

Implement the adaptive AI locally.

Recommended prediction model:

* weighted recent action frequency
* transition table
* recency weighting
* simple greed score
* simple bluff/contrarian tendency

The model should be explainable and deterministic enough to document.

Do not call this a neural network or large language model.

BALANCING REQUIREMENTS

The player should NOT be able to:

* always follow predictions and trivially win
* always bluff and trivially win
* always choose one direction and trivially win
* discover a simple "always choose opposite" solution

The AI should therefore adapt to:

* greed
* repetition
* contrarian behaviour

The first three levels should be relatively welcoming.

After Level 3, difficulty should increase through:

* stronger predictions
* larger reward gaps when confidence is high
* stronger TRACE consequences
* more sophisticated behavioural modeling
* room challenges

Do not simply multiply enemy difficulty or reduce reward randomly.

DESIGN PRIORITY

The player must understand:

1. I need points to escape.
2. The AI prediction has the highest immediate reward.
3. Following the prediction makes me easier to understand.
4. Bluffing sacrifices immediate score but protects me.
5. The AI learns how I play.
6. Later rooms impose additional rules designed around my behaviour.

If any visual or system element does not help communicate those six ideas, do not add it.
